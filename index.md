**Janus** is the Unigrid Control Center, the desktop wallet. It downloads Hedgehog and shows your legacy wallet's balance and history.
Downloads are on the [Janus releases page](https://github.com/unigrid-project/janus-java/releases).

**Hedgehog** is the node software of the Unigrid network.
Downloads are on the [Hedgehog releases page](https://github.com/unigrid-project/hedgehog/releases).

**Governance** holds the Unigrid DAO constitution and the proposals voted on so far.
